# RQ1: Flannery Opening Frequency

## Research question

How frequently does a randomly dealt hand qualify for our proposed Flannery 2♦ opening, what is the distribution of qualifying shapes and strength ranges, and what percentage of ordinary 1♥ openings does Flannery remove?

## Intended outputs

This experiment is intended to produce:

- the overall frequency of qualifying Flannery hands among randomly dealt hands;
- the equivalent "one qualifying hand every N hands" rate;
- counts and percentages by qualifying shape;
- counts and percentages by strength range;
- the share of ordinary 1♥ openings that would instead be opened 2♦ under the proposed Flannery definition;
- 95% confidence intervals for the main frequency estimates;
- machine-readable summary output suitable for the project tracker and book evidence notes.

## Definition status

The proposed Flannery definition is intentionally left as a placeholder until it is frozen in `config.json`. The experiment should not be run for publication until that definition, the ordinary 1♥ baseline, the sample size, and the confidence interval method are all explicit.
