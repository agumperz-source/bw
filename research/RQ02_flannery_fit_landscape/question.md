# RQ2: Flannery Fit Landscape

## Research question

After a qualifying Flannery 2♦ opening, what major-suit fit and responder-strength landscape does opener's partner actually hold?

## Intended outputs

This experiment measures, among qualifying Flannery openers from the same deterministic Monte Carlo sample used in RQ1:

- responder heart-length distribution;
- responder spade-length distribution;
- heart-fit distribution by opener hearts and responder hearts;
- spade-fit distribution by opener spades and responder spades;
- frequency of 8+ card heart fits;
- frequency of 8+ card spade fits;
- frequency of no 8-card major fit;
- responder HCP distribution and common value thresholds.

The purpose is to connect the frequency result to system design: what responder needs to sort out after 2♦ is opened.
